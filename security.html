The Morris Worm: A Turning Point in Cybersecurity

The Morris Worm was released on November 2, 1988. It spread across computer networks and affected thousands of computers, disrupting their normal operation. This incident was significant because it demonstrated how quickly malicious software could spread and helped raise awareness of the importance of computer security.
