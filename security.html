WannaCry: A Worldwide Ransomware Attack

The WannaCry ransomware attack occurred on May 12, 2017. The attack spread quickly across vunerable Windows computers, encrypted files, and demanded ransom payments from victims. It affected hospitals, businesses, and individuals worldwide, including the United Kingdom's National Health Service (NHS). This attack was prominent in cybersecurity history because it demonstrated the dangers of unpatched software vulnerabilities and the importance of security updates and reliable data backups.
