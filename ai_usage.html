AI should be used as a supportive tool for students. I should not replace the thinking of students, because it would not be the students own thoughts or work.

Incorrect AI Usage

[First incorrect use of AI]
Academic Dishonesty: Using AI to complete coursework that requires your own thinking. Such as using AI on quizzess, test, or having AI complete an entire assignment when it is not allowed by the instructor or institution.
[Second incorrect use of AI]
Evidence Reliance: Relying on evidence that AI provides is not ethical. The evidence and research must be done on your own to ensure that it is true. If not, it could result in spreading false information.
[Third incorrect use of AI]
Plagiarism: Submitting AI-generated assignments without authorization or acknolegement is not honest.

I believe that AI is helpful, but it cannot be relied upon.
Using AI to complete assingments without permission or acknoledgement of doing so, can lead to academic dishonesty.
Students should always aim to be responsibile and honest when using AI resources.
AI should be used as a learning tool over a tool that replaces the raw thinking of stduents.
